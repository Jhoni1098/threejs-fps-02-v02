FROSTLINE

A round-based tactical FPS on a frozen harbour. Aurora attacks — fight through the ice and containers to the east warehouse, reach the hostage, and escort them back to the green dock ring. Breakwater defends. First team to 5 rounds takes the match. Money earned each round buys better guns between rounds.

  • WASD Move
  • Mouse Look · Click Fire
  • R Reload
  • Shift Walk (steady aim)
  • Ctrl Crouch
  • Space Jump
  • B Buy menu (between rounds)
  • 1–4 Weapons · G Grenade
  • Esc Pause

BUY PHASE

Spend your round money before the round starts. Click an item or press its number key.

Press B or click Close to return. Weapons you own stay in your loadout for the match.
